Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

Footvolley Kings 3D ("the game") is published by Idan Lavi, Hativat Harel Street, Modi'in-Maccabim-Re'ut, Israel ("we", "us"). We are responsible for the personal data described in this policy.

Contact for privacy questions and requests: ninjago6@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • We use your data to run the game: your progress, online matches, friends, rankings and account.
  • On iPhone you can choose to watch a short rewarded ad to shorten a victory chest's unlock timer. It is always optional: the game never shows an ad you did not ask for, and an ad never gives Coins, Gems or chests.
  • Those ads are not personalised, and the game never asks to track you (no App Tracking Transparency prompt).
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • If you buy Gems, Coins, the starter bundle or Season Pass Premium, Apple processes the payment. We never see your card or bank details. We keep a record of each purchase so that it is delivered exactly once and refunds work (section 3.5).
  • The game contains no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK. (Google's ad software on iPhone collects its own data: section 3.4.)
  • You can see what our servers keep about you, and you can delete your account and its data, from inside the game: Settings › Account.

3.3 Data processed by our service provider, Unity

The game's online features run on Unity Gaming Services, operated by Unity Technologies.

IP address. When the game connects, Unity's services see your IP address. Unity's documentation says that:

  • Unity Relay keeps the IP address and player ID for 30 days;
  • Unity Lobby keeps the player ID in its logs for up to 30 days.

Our own server code does not store IP addresses.

Approximate location. Unity's services work out an approximate (coarse) location from the connection, to choose a nearby game server region. The region chosen for a match is shared with the other players in that match.

Match traffic. Online match traffic goes through Unity Relay servers, encrypted. The game does not connect directly to other players' devices.

Unity's own declarations for its software (Apple privacy manifests) list further data types for its sign-in software. These include basic diagnostic and usage data, which is not used for tracking. See section 7.

3.4 Optional rewarded ads on iPhone (Google AdMob)

The iPhone version contains Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and Google's consent tool (User Messaging Platform). They are used only for the optional WATCH AD button on an unlocking victory chest.

What Google's software collects. Google documents that its Mobile Ads SDK collects, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

  • device identifiers (app- or developer-scoped identifiers; the advertising identifier only if you allowed tracking, and this game never asks);
  • approximate location derived from the IP address;
  • ad interactions, such as ads viewed and taps;
  • diagnostics and performance data.

How the game limits it. Every ad request is marked for teen treatment: no personalised ads, no remarketing, and Google's ad protections for teens. The maximum ad content rating is T (teen). The game never requests the advertising identifier.

Google's role. Google acts as an independent controller of the data it uses for ads, under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Confirming a watched ad. Google sends a signed confirmation to a small web address we run on Cloudflare (our service provider), which passes it to our game server. The server checks Google's signature before it changes any timer.

3.5 In-app purchases (Apple)

On iPhone and iPad you can buy Gem packs, Coin packs, a one-time starter bundle and Season Pass Premium with real money, through Apple's In-App Purchase. Purchases need a secured account (a username and password, or email, Google or Apple sign-in), so that what you buy is never lost with a device.

Apple processes the payment. Apple handles your payment details under its own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. We never receive your card or bank details, your Apple Account, your name or your email address from Apple.

What we receive from Apple. When you buy something, Apple gives the game a purchase record signed by Apple. Our server checks Apple's signature and reads:

  • the transaction ID, the product and the purchase date;
  • the App Store country;
  • whether it was a real or a test (sandbox) purchase;
  • an account token that the game makes from your player ID, so the purchase is credited to your account and no other.

Apple's record also contains the price and currency; our server does not store them.

What we keep.

  • For each purchase: the transaction ID, the product, the dates, exactly what it gave you (Gems, Coins, items or Premium), its state (delivered, refunded or restored) and the history of any refund or restore.
  • A register that links each purchase to the account that received it, under a one-way code made from the transaction ID. It makes sure that a purchase is delivered only once, and it lets a refund find the right account.
  • Entries in your currency ledger (section 3.2), the number of refunds in the last 365 days, and whether purchases are paused or locked (see the Terms of Service, section 7).

Refund notices from Apple. Apple sends notices about refunds and other purchase events (App Store Server Notifications) to a web address we run on Cloudflare (our service provider). They contain Apple's signed purchase details, including the account token. Our server checks Apple's signature, applies a refund once (Terms of Service, section 7), and ignores notices that do not change anything.

Why.

  • To deliver what you paid for exactly once, and to restore it if something goes wrong.
  • To apply refunds that Apple grants.
  • To prevent fraud, such as one purchase being credited twice.
  • To answer support requests about purchases.

We are based in Israel. The European Commission recognises Israel as providing an adequate level of protection for personal data. The information you send while playing goes directly to our service providers, which may store or process it in the United States and other countries outside the one you live in, where data-protection laws may differ from those of your country. Where the GDPR or the UK GDPR applies and a provider is in a country without an adequacy decision, the following safeguards apply.

Unity Technologies (game servers, online matches, sign-in).

  • What: your account, progress, saves, ranked records and friends data, and the online match traffic.
  • Where: stored and processed with Unity Gaming Services. Unity does not offer us a choice of storage region, so we do not state one. Relay servers for a match are chosen near the players (section 3.3).
  • Role: for the game services Unity runs for us (sign-in, cloud saves, ranked play, friends and multiplayer), Unity is our processor under its Data Processing Addendum. For Unity Player Accounts (section 5), Unity is an independent controller under its own privacy policy.
  • Safeguards: the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, with the UK Addendum and the Swiss amendments: https://unity.com/legal/unity-data-processing-addendum-dpa. Unity's sub-processors are listed at https://unity.com/legal/subprocessors.

Google (optional rewarded ads on iPhone).

  • Role: an independent controller (section 3.4).
  • Where: Google states that it maintains servers around the world.
  • Safeguards: Google's Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms, relying on adequacy decisions, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. framework) and Standard Contractual Clauses: https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks.

Apple (in-app purchases).

  • Role: Apple processes the payment as an independent controller, under its own privacy policy (section 3.5).

Cloudflare (ad confirmations and Apple's purchase notices).

  • Where: the web address that receives Google's ad confirmations and Apple's purchase notices runs on Cloudflare's worldwide network. It stores them in a Cloudflare database that we created in Cloudflare's Western Europe region: ad confirmations for 7 days, Apple's notices for 30 days.
  • Role: our processor.
  • Safeguards: the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework (with its UK Extension and the Swiss-U.S. framework), and the Standard Contractual Clauses in Cloudflare's Data Processing Addendum if that framework stops applying: https://www.cloudflare.com/cloudflare-customer-dpa/.

You can ask for a copy of these safeguards at ninjago6@gmail.com. We may remove commercial details before sending them.

7. Advertising, tracking and analytics

Advertising: optional rewarded ads on iPhone only (section 3.4).

  • An ad plays only when you tap WATCH AD on an unlocking victory chest.
  • Each ad that Google confirms takes 30 minutes off that chest's timer, at most 3 ads per chest. Nothing else is ever given for an ad.
  • There are no banners, no ads between matches and no forced ads.

Consent. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your permission before Google stores or reads information on your device. If you agree, Google may use it to show non-personalised ads, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud. If you do not agree, Google shows limited ads that do not use such information. Where these choices apply to you, you can change them at any time in Settings › PRIVACY & TERMS › AD PRIVACY CHOICES.

Tracking. The game does not track you across other apps or websites. It never shows Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and never requests the advertising identifier. Ads are not personalised.

Analytics. The game has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK. The game's Unity analytics, crash and performance services are switched off.

Unity's sign-in software declares in its Apple privacy manifest that it may collect basic usage and crash data to operate the sign-in service; it is not used for tracking.

9. Children

Footvolley Kings 3D is for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Because some of our players are teenagers, every ad request is treated as for a minor: no personalised ads, and only ad content suitable for teens (section 3.4).

Players under 18 need a parent's or guardian's permission to make purchases. Apple's Family Sharing ("Ask to Buy") and Screen Time let a parent approve or block purchases.

Unity Player Accounts, an optional way to sign in that is run by Unity, is not intended for children unless a parent has given verified consent where the law requires it. If you are under the age at which you can consent to online services on your own in your country (between 13 and 16 in the EU), please play as a guest or use a username and password instead.

If you believe that a child under 13 has given us personal data, email ninjago6@gmail.com and we will delete it.

No system is perfectly secure.
