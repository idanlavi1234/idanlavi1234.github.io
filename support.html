Support

Contact us

Email FOOTvolleyKING@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 7 days.

Please include:

  • your player ID (in the game: Settings › Account › YOUR PLAYER ID, with a COPY button);
  • your device and its operating system;
  • what happened.

Never send us your password.

Common questions

Keep my progress on a new phone. Secure your player in Settings › Account, with email, Google or Apple, or with a username. Then sign in on the new device.

Report a player. Open the match in your ranked History and tap REPORT PLAYER. You can also block a player in Friends.

Delete my account. See Delete your account.

Privacy. See the Privacy Policy. Privacy requests: ninjago6@gmail.com.

Ads. On iPhone, ads play only when you choose WATCH AD on an unlocking victory chest. Where they apply, your ad privacy choices are in Settings › PRIVACY & TERMS › AD PRIVACY CHOICES.

Purchases. Gem packs, Coin packs, the starter bundle and Season Pass Premium are bought through Apple. If something you paid for is missing, email us with your player ID (shown under Settings › Account). Refunds for App Store purchases are requested from Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com; what happens in the game after a refund is described in the Terms of Service, section 7.
