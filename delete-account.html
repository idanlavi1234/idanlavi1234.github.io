Delete your account

Delete in the game (fastest)

  1. Open Settings › Account. The PRIVACY & TERMS screen also has DELETE ACCOUNT.
  2. Tap DELETE THIS ACCOUNT…, read what will be deleted, type DELETE, and tap the red button twice.
  3. The game deletes your data from every game server, then your sign-in, then your progress on that device.

The deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Ask us to delete it (if you no longer have the game)

  1. Email FOOTvolleyKING@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Include your player ID if you have it. It is shown in the game under Settings › Account (YOUR PLAYER ID). Also include anything that helps us find the account: your display name, or your friend code.
  3. A player ID alone is not enough: we will tell you by email what proof of ownership we need for your account, and we delete nothing until we are satisfied that the account is yours. We will not ask for more information than we need. If your account is secured (email, Google, Apple or a username), the quickest way is to install the game again, sign in, and delete the account in the game (see above).
  4. Once we have verified that the account is yours, we delete it within 30 days, and we email you when it is done. If we cannot verify it, we will tell you why.

A guest player without a player ID. If you never secured your guest player and you no longer have the game, we may be unable to find which account was yours. In that case we cannot delete it on request.

What is deleted

  • Your progress, currencies, items, Season Pass, missions, achievements and chests on our servers.
  • Your saves and backups.
  • Your ranked record and leaderboard rows.
  • Your friends list, requests, invites, team and blocks.
  • Device reports you sent.
  • Your player ID and its sign-ins.

What is kept, and why

Some records stay, but only under a one-way code that cannot be linked back to you:

  • Purchase records, kept against fraud and for refunds: which purchase was delivered, and its state, until 24 months after the purchase date. If Apple refunds a purchase after your account is deleted, the refund is recorded only under that code.
  • Administrative log entries.
  • Your place in other players' match results, shown as "DELETED PLAYER".

A friend code is never given to anyone else.

Email, Google or Apple sign-in

Signing in with email, Google or Apple creates a Unity Player Account, which belongs to Unity and can also be used by other games. Delete it on Unity's page: https://player-account.unity.com/. Unity's privacy contact is unity-player-login-privacy@unity3d.com.
